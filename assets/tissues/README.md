## Layout and route rationale

- Ingestion: start outside the lips, enter the oral cavity above the tongue, pass through the pharynx, and descend through the illustrated esophagus behind the trachea.
- Stomach: follow the open lumen, curve with its J-shaped cavity, then leave through the illustrated pyloric outlet.
- Intestinal lining: move through luminal space, cross the illustrated epithelium once, and join the underlying vessel.
- Portal circulation: follow a tributary into the broad collecting vessel.
- Liver: enter the illustrated portal vessel and follow its branching course into the cutaway.
- Tissue-colored digestive passages and blue blood-vessel passages connect the enlarged views. Cross-page transitions occur between sections. Text remains in the opposite column on desktop and below the figure on narrow screens. A side lane clears the captions and mobile copy.
- The Yakult bottle remains the visual guide throughout and shrinks before the tissue journey. It represents the conceptual dose journey; it is not a literal bottle entering tissue. This illustration does not establish liver delivery of Renata's engineered LCA-sulfation system.
